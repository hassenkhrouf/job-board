# Project Vision

## Problem

Many job seekers search daily for:
- Private sector jobs
- Government competitions
- Internships
- Remote opportunities

Information is scattered across many websites and social networks.

## Solution

Create a centralized platform where users can quickly find opportunities.

## Business Model

Primary revenue:
- Google AdSense
- Sponsored job posts
- Featured listings

## Success Metrics

Main KPIs:

- Organic Google traffic
- Number of indexed pages
- Daily visitors
- Advertisement revenue
